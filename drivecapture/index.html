DriveCapture

A small macOS menu bar app: press a shortcut, capture a screenshot or screen recording, and it is uploaded to a Google Drive folder you choose — the link is copied to your clipboard.

What it does

  • Global shortcuts for region / full-screen screenshots and screen recordings.
  • Uploads the capture to the Google Drive folder you pick in Settings, using your own Google account.
  • Creates a view link for the uploaded file and copies it to the clipboard.

Why it asks for Google Drive access

DriveCapture needs Google Drive access to list your folders (so you can choose where captures go), upload the files you capture, and set a link-sharing permission on those files. It does not read, modify or delete any other files.

Privacy

There is no DriveCapture server. Your sign-in tokens stay on your Mac and your files go directly to your own Google Drive. Read the privacy policy.
