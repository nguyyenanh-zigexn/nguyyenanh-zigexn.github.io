Privacy Policy

DriveCapture · Last updated: September 28, 2026

DriveCapture is a macOS app that captures screenshots and screen recordings on your Mac and uploads them to your own Google Drive. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • Screen content — only when you press a capture shortcut. Captures are saved on your Mac (by default in ~/Movies/DriveCapture) and uploaded to the Google Drive folder you selected.
  • Microphone / system audio — only if you enable audio recording in Settings, and only during a recording.
  • Google account data — your email address (to show which account is signed in), the list of your Drive folders (so you can choose a destination), and the files DriveCapture uploads.

How Google user data is used

DriveCapture uses Google Drive access solely to: list folders so you can choose where captures are saved; upload the captures you make; and add a link-sharing permission to those uploaded files so the copied link can be opened. It does not read, download, modify or delete any other files in your Drive, and it does not use Google user data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than providing these features.

DriveCapture's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

  • There is no DriveCapture server. The app talks directly from your Mac to Google's APIs.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on your Mac, in a file readable only by your user account.
  • No data is sent to the developer or any third party, and no data is sold.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time in DriveCapture → Settings → Account; this removes the stored tokens.
  • Revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete local captures from ~/Movies/DriveCapture and uploaded files from your Google Drive.
